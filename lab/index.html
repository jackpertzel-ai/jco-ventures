Pertzel Lab.

An independent research practice run by Jack, J & Co's founder. It asks how open physiological-signal AI models travel from clinical datasets toward consumer sensors, one six-week season at a time, and publishes the code, the figures and the record of how it went at the end of each season. It reports what the data shows, never health advice.

Season 1 · 12 October to 22 November 2026 · Gate review 27 November

How well does an open ECG model travel?

Week 1 check passed, 13 October Week 2 check met, 21 October Model: ECGFounder, published weights, run unchanged No accuracy claim yet
  1. 01Set up and prove the pipeline: the model runs on ten PTB-XL records on a laptop. Done.
  2. 02Reproduce the authors' published PTB-XL score, with the tolerance written down before the run. Done: finding by finding it reproduces (29 of 30 within 0.001); the stated average does not follow from the paper's own table, and the gap is documented.
  3. 03Run the model on VitalDB, surgery recordings it has never seen, on the screened segments where every beat was checked by at least two anaesthesiologists.
  4. 04Report the transfer gap: discrimination, calibration and subgroups.
  5. 05Publish the code, a preprint, the figures and the weekly record, including what broke.

Rules

How the lab works.

Stated before the runs
  • Tolerances, label mappings and subgroups are written down before a run, never after.
  • Every number traces to a run folder with its configuration and outputs. No run folder, no number.
  • Open data and open weights, with the code published alongside each season's result and each dataset's licence respected and quoted.
  • Failures are published alongside results. A documented gap is a result.
  • The lab reports what a signal, a sensor or a model shows, never what it means for anyone's health.
