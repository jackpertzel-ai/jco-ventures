§3 Drawn by the person behind the company

Jack Pertzel.

Founder · Melbourne

J & Co is founded and run by Jack Pertzel. He started by running a small disability-services provider — living its administrative burden personally, then building the software to carry it. That turned into a company that builds in two directions: confidential AI for organisations with sensitive work, and precision instruments anyone can afford and verify.

Jack is on his way to medicine; the endgame is a clinic that is also a lab. The company is how he gets there — one finished, useful object at a time.
