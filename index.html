ABOUT

I'm Jack Pertzel. I build instruments that give people control over things normally controlled by institutions — their data, their money, their health, their compliance obligations.

I started by running a small NDIS provider, which meant living the administrative burden personally, then building the software to carry it. That turned into building in two directions: confidential AI for organisations that can't hand sensitive work to a hyperscaler, and precision sensing instruments anyone can afford and verify.

I'm on the way to medicine — the endgame is a clinic that is also a lab. Everything here is a step toward that.

I ship one finished, documented, publicly usable thing every six weeks. I'd rather show you an object than a deck.
